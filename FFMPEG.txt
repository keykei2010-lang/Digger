Video previews in the portable Windows build use FFmpeg through the
PyAV 16.1.0 Windows x64 wheel for CPython 3.13. The bundled DLLs come from
that wheel. The wheel includes FFmpeg built with GPL components, including
x264 and x265. See the wheel build recipes for dependency sources.
